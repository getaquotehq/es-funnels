Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Energy Saver Hub, operated by The Pirotta Group ("we", "us"), respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and your choices. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

When you book a free site visit and quote, we collect:

  • Contact details: your name, email address, phone number, suburb and postcode.
  • Your answers: what you are interested in, whether you have solar or a battery (and, if you do, its approximate age and your feed-in tariff), your approximate quarterly bill, whether you own your home, and your timeframe.
  • Your electricity bill (optional): if you upload one, it may show your address, account and meter details, tariff, and energy usage.
  • How you found us: advertising campaign details from the link you clicked (such as UTM tags or a Facebook click ID), the page you came from, and the page address.

You do not have to give us this information, but without it we cannot arrange your site visit or quote.

How we use it

  • To contact you for a short discovery call and to arrange your site visit.
  • To assess your roof, bills, usage and needs, and to prepare advice and quotes.
  • To arrange and carry out any installation you go ahead with, and to provide warranty support.
  • To keep records of our dealings with you.
  • To understand which of our advertising works, so we can improve it.

We may contact you by phone, email or SMS about your enquiry. We will only send you other marketing if you have agreed to it, and you can opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed with:

  • Our installers: the local certified electricians who carry out your site visit and installation.
  • Supabase (secure database and file storage for form submissions and bills).
  • QuoteLeadsHQ (the system we use to manage enquiries and follow-ups).
  • Make.com (automation that moves enquiries between our systems).
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram), which measures whether our adverts lead to enquiries, through the Meta Pixel described below.

Some of these providers may store or process information on servers outside Australia, including in the United States, the European Union or Asia. We choose reputable providers that protect data with security measures appropriate to its sensitivity.

We may also disclose information where required or authorised by law, or to product manufacturers and distributors when needed to register or claim a warranty for equipment we install for you.

How we protect it

Uploaded bills are kept in private storage that cannot be accessed from the public website. Access to enquiries and bills is limited to people who need it to help you. Information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to deal with your enquiry, provide our services, and meet our legal and warranty obligations. When it is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

Cookies, the Meta Pixel and third party services

This website uses the Meta Pixel, which may set cookies and tell Meta when you view the page or submit an enquiry. Meta uses this to measure and improve our adverts, under its own privacy policy. You can manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings, and block cookies in your browser.

The page also loads fonts from Google Fonts and code from a content delivery network (jsDelivr), which means those services receive your IP address when the page loads, as with most websites.

Accessing or correcting your information

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time, usually 30 days.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to fix it. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.
